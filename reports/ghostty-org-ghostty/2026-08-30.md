[^fn1]: “All AI usage in any form must be disclosed. You must state the tool you used (e.g. Claude Code, Cursor, Amp) along with the extent that the work was AI-assisted.” [`AI_POLICY.md:5`](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/8af6897c0afc63037a8a3efee4162a380e3a4572/AI_POLICY.md#L5)
[^fn2]: Applies only to outside contributions (PRs, issues, discussions) to Ghostty; maintainers are exempt and may use AI at their discretion. AI usage must be disclosed (tool used and extent of AI assistance) and the human contributor must fully understand all code/content before submitting.
[^fn3]: “No AI-generated media is allowed (art, images, videos, audio, etc.). Text and code are the only acceptable AI-generated content, per the other rules in this policy.” [`AI_POLICY.md:21`](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/8af6897c0afc63037a8a3efee4162a380e3a4572/AI_POLICY.md#L21)
[^fn4]: AI-generated media (art, images, videos, audio) is banned entirely; text content is treated as falling under the same disclosure/human-in-the-loop rules as code contributions.
[^fn5]: “People who produce bad contributions that are clearly AI (slop) will be added to our public denouncement list. This list will block all future contributions.” [`AI_POLICY.md:25`](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/8af6897c0afc63037a8a3efee4162a380e3a4572/AI_POLICY.md#L25)
[^fn6]: Contributors who repeatedly submit low-quality/'AI slop' work are added to a public denouncement list that blocks all future contributions from them; the list may be shared with other projects.
